# Who Is Robbie Cumming's Partner?

Robbie Cumming is the presenter and creator behind Canal Boat Diaries, a BBC and U&Yesterday series that follows his life aboard his 42-foot narrowboat, Naughty Lass. He has lived on the boat for more than a decade, and beyond the show he also runs a podcast and touring live show called Canal Boat Stories.

For most of his time in the public eye, Robbie kept his personal relationships private, even while documenting much of his day-to-day life on camera. That changed in June 2026, when his relationship with a woman named Lyndsey became public after she appeared in the series 7 finale. Robbie later shared the news himself on Instagram, saying he could now go Instagram official, and posted photos of the two of them. The pair reportedly share an interest in rock and metal music. They are not married and have no publicly known children together.

Robbie has spoken about a few past relationships over the years, including a girlfriend in London around 2012 to 2013 and someone named Alice around 2022. Compared to those years of privacy, the public confirmation of his relationship with Lyndsey stood out as a notably personal moment for someone who otherwise shares so much of his life with viewers.
